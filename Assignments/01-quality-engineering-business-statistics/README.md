# 01 — Quality Engineering & Business Statistics

Project report and study guide built on the course's Testing framework (`testing-framework.jpeg`) and Chapter 10, *Quality and Quality Control*, of Levin & Rubin, *Statistics for Management*.

## Covers
1. **Quality Attributes Framework**: how Testing splits into Quality Control and Quality Assurance
2. **Quality Control**: simulation methods and tools, graphical views (x̄, R, p and c charts), results
3. **Quality Assurance**: inspection and review, change management (RFC, RFM), testing methods, test cases, testing oracle, workflow checklist, results
4. Scenario and analytical practice questions
5. Solved numericals: Chapters 3–16 self-check exercises, with Chapter 10 as the main focus (control charts, TQM and Pareto analysis, acceptance sampling and OC curves)
6. Required diagrams: Testing framework flowchart, SPC dashboard, OC curve
